## 33(5) MHADA Development/Redevelopment {#335}

**Land**: MHADA lands only
**FSI**: 3.0 on gross plot area (exclusive of fungible)
**Enhanced FSI**: 4.0 for plots ≥ 4000 sqm with road ≥ 18m (Govt approval required)
**Minimum BUA**: 60% must be EWS/LIG/MIG tenements

### Rehab Entitlement
- Basic: Existing carpet + 35%, minimum 35 sqm per tenement
- **If existing ≥ 35 sqm**: carpet × (1 + 35% + layout%) → × 1.2 (BUA) → × 1.35 (fungible) = MOFA
- **If existing < 35 sqm**: bumped to 35 sqm, gets ONLY layout% (NOT 35% entitlement) → × 1.2 (BUA) → × 1.35 (fungible) = MOFA
- Layout entitlement by plot size (only for plots ≥ 4000 sqm):

| Plot Area | Layout Additional % |
|-----------|-------------------------------|
| Below 4000 sqm | 0% (no layout benefit) |
| 4000 sqm to 2 ha | 15% |
| 2 ha to 5 ha | 25% |
| 5 ha to 10 ha | 35% |
| Above 10 ha | 45% |

- Commercial/amenity units: existing carpet + 20%

Two options:
- **Option A (Housing Stock Sharing)**: Additional 1.0 FSI shared as housing stock
- **Option B (Premium)**: Pay premium at % of ASR based on LR/RC and category (EWS/LIG/MIG/HIG)

## 33(7) Cessed Building Redevelopment {#337}

**Land**: Island City only
**Building**: Cessed buildings existing prior to 30/09/1969
**FSI**: 3.0 on gross plot area OR rehab + incentive, whichever is more

### Rehab Entitlement
- Each tenant: minimum 27.88 sqm, maximum 120 sqm carpet
- Entitlement = MAX(existing carpet area, 27.88 sqm)
- Additional area per tenant based on number of plots:
  - Single plot: +5% of entitlement
  - 2-5 plots combined: +8%
  - 6+ plots combined: +15%
- **MOFA conversion**: Entitlement carpet × 1.2 (BUA) × 1.35 (fungible) = MOFA area

- MHADA gets prescribed percentage of surplus BUA as per MHAD Act
- Remaining surplus to developer/society

### Key Premiums
- Development cess: 100% of development charges OR ₹5000/sqm (whichever applicable)
- Corpus fund for maintenance
- 10% relaxation premium for open space deficiency
- Fungible: Free on rehab, 50% RR on sale/incentive

## 33(7A) Dilapidated Building Redevelopment {#337a}

**Land**: Suburbs/Extended Suburbs (tenant-occupied buildings) OR Island City (non-cessed tenant-occupied)
**Building**: Must be dilapidated/unsafe OR 30+ years old
**FSI**: As per Regulation 30(A) Table 12 (road-width dependent, e.g., 2.7 for 27m road)
**Computed on**: Net Plot Area (Gross - road setback - reservations)

### Rehab Entitlement
- Each tenant: minimum 27.88 sqm, maximum 120 sqm carpet
- Entitlement = MAX(existing carpet, 27.88 sqm)
- Additional area per tenant based on number of plots:
  - Single plot: +5% of entitlement
  - 2-5 plots combined: +8%
  - 6+ plots combined: +15%
- **MOFA conversion**: Entitlement carpet × 1.2 (BUA) × 1.35 (fungible) = MOFA area

### 33(7A) Entitlement Examples
Same calculation as 33(7) — MAX(existing, 27.88) × (1 + additional%) then ×1.2 → ×1.35 → ÷1.2 = MOFA.
See 33(7) examples table above for full worked scenarios.

### Incentive FSI
- 70% of rehab FSI consumption (standard for 33(7A))
- All goes to developer

### Surplus
- All surplus to developer/society (no MHADA share)
- This is the key advantage over 33(7) — no mandatory sharing

### Key Premiums
- ₹5000/sqm development cess
- 50% premium on additional FSI (above zonal)
- 10% relaxation premium for open space deficiency
- Fungible: Free on rehab, 50% RR on sale
- Staircase premium: 15% of construction cost

## 33(7B) Housing Society Redevelopment {#337b}

**Land**: Any location (excluding buildings covered under 33(7) and 33(7A))
**Building**: Existing residential housing societies, 30+ years old
**FSI**: As per Regulation 30(A) Table 12
**Computed on**: Net Plot Area

### Rehab Entitlement
- Existing BUA of the society (members re-accommodated at existing area)

### Incentive FSI
- 15% of existing BUA OR 10 sqm per tenement, whichever is more
- This is WITHOUT premium
- Can additionally avail FSI/TDR up to permissible 30(A) limit

### Surplus
- All surplus to society/developer (no MHADA share)

### Key Premiums
- Standard premiums as per Regulation 30(A)
- Fungible: Free on existing BUA component, 50% RR on balance

## 33(9) Cluster Development Scheme (CDS) {#339}

**Land**: Any (mixed tenure allowed — MHADA + private + slum can combine)
**FSI**: 4.0 on gross plot area OR rehab + incentive, whichever is more
**Min Plot**: 4000 sqm (City) / 6000 sqm (Suburbs)
**Min Road**: 18m (or 12m dead-end within 50m of 18m road)

### Rehab Entitlement
- Residential: min 27.88 sqm, max 120 sqm per tenant
- Non-residential: existing area or 20.90 sqm, whichever is less
- Additional area by cluster size:

| 5000 sqm - 1 ha | 15% |
| 1-2 ha | 20% |
| 2-5 ha | 25% |
| 5-10 ha | 30% |
| Above 10 ha | 35% |

- **MOFA conversion**: Entitlement carpet × (1 + cluster additional%) × 1.2 (BUA) × 1.35 (fungible) = MOFA area

## 33(10) SRA Slum Rehabilitation {#3310}

**Land**: Declared/notified slum areas
**FSI**: Up to 4.0 (varies by density and location)
**Computed on**: Net plot area (as per SRA computation)
**Authority**: SRA (Slum Rehabilitation Authority)

### Eligibility
- Protected occupiers under Chapter I-B of Slum Act
- 51% consent of eligible hutment dwellers required
- Censused in 1976, 1980, 1985, or prior to 1/1/1995, 1/1/2000

### Rehab Entitlement
- 27.88 sqm carpet per eligible hutment dweller (residential)
- 20.90 sqm for commercial/pavement dwellers
- Even those with > 27.88 sqm only get 27.88 sqm
- Joint ownership with spouse mandatory

### FSI Distribution
- Rehab component + free-sale component
- Incentive: 1.25x of rehab area = sale incentive (typical)
- Density-based: 650 hutments/hectare determines rehab count

### Key Premiums
- Land premium: 25% of ASR (for Govt/MCGM/MHADA land)
- Infra charges: ₹840/sqm on BUA above zonal FSI
- Corpus fund: ₹40,000 per tenement
- Fungible: Free on rehab, as per SRA norms on sale

### Special Provisions
- Lease: 30 years renewable, rent ₹1001 per 4000 sqm/year
- Auto-termination of vacant land tenure on LOI issuance
- 10-year transfer restriction on rehab tenements
- Transit accommodation mandatory during construction

## 33(11) Permanent Transit Camp (PTC) {#3311}

**Land**: Any non-reserved, non-SDZ land
**FSI**: Up to 4.0 total
**Purpose**: Construction of permanent transit camp tenements for SRA

### FSI Distribution

| Location | Road Width | Total FSI | Zonal FSI | Additional | PTC % | Sale % |
|----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|------------|-------|--------|
| Island City | 12m | 3.0 | 1.33 | 1.67 | 63% | 37% |
| Island City | 18m | 4.0 | 1.33 | 2.67 | 50% | 50% |
| Suburbs | 12m | 3.0 | 1.00 | 2.00 | 63% | 37% |
| Suburbs | 18m | 4.0 | 1.00 | 3.00 | 50% | 50% |

### PTC Tenement Specs
- Residential: 27.88 sqm carpet
- Commercial (ground floor): 20.90 sqm for project-affected commercial tenants
- Handed over FREE to SRA

### Clubbing Provision
- Can club multiple plots (same/adjoining ward or within 5 km)
- PTC on one plot, sale on another
- Unearned income: 40% of difference in ASR sale value of shifted BUA
- Premium: 50% at IOA, 50% at CC

### PTC Cost Calculation
- Source rate: ASR rate of transit camp source location
- Destination rate: ASR rate of PTC construction location
- PTC cost = (number of PTC tenements) × (carpet × loading) × construction cost per sqm

### Key Premiums
- Standard fungible (50% RR) on sale component
- 2/3 of total premium → shelter fund (State)
- 1/3 → Deputy Director of Town Planning

## 33(12B) Tolerated Structure Re-accommodation {#3312b}

**Land**: Any (TP Scheme area, structures on road alignments)
**FSI**: Permissible FSI + additional (50% of re-accommodated structure area)
**Cap**: Overall FSI of final plot ≤ 4.0

### Eligibility
- Residential: structures existing prior to 17/04/1964
- Non-residential: existing prior to 01/04/1962
- Must be reflected in Revenue Records prior to 1961-62

### Rehab
- 27.88 sqm per tolerated residential occupant
- Commercial: existing area or 20.90 sqm (whichever less)
- BUA up to 27.88 sqm: free to tenant; balance: mutually agreed amount

### Developer Incentive
- 50% of total area of structures covered (re-accommodated)
- Subject to 4.0 FSI cap

### Requirements
- Tri-party registered agreement (developer + plot owner + occupiers)
- Advance possession receipt to MCGM
- Plot owner eligible for TDR at zonal FSI for land handed over

## 33(19) CBD Commercial FSI {#3319}

**Land**: CBD zone, commercial zone, or independent plot converted from industrial
**FSI**: Up to 5.0 (including 30(A) permissible)
**Computed on**: Plot area excluding reservations

### FSI by Road Width (Commercial Zone / Residential Zone plots)

| Min Road Width | Max FSI |
|---------------|---------|
| 12m | 3.0 |
| 18m | 4.0 |
| 27m | 5.0 |

### Key Conditions
- Maximum 30% residential from 30(A) FSI only
- Additional FSI NOT permitted for residential
- No Inclusive Housing (IH) requirement in CBD
- Premium: 50% of ASR for open developed land (FSI 1), shared equally GoM/MCGM

### Restrictions
- Not for reserved/designated plots (except DP road/RL/parking reservation)
- Applies after Reg 14(B) compliance

## Common Calculation Parameters {#common}

### Conversion Factor (MOFA Carpet → BUA)
- **MOFA Carpet → BUA: × 1.20** (official IGR Maharashtra multiplier)
- Used by: IGR for stamp duty valuation, DCPR 2034 for FSI computation, BMC sanctioned plans
- NOT a thumb rule — this is the standardised government conversion factor
- **RERA Carpet → BUA: × 1.10** (RERA includes internal walls, excludes external)
- **MOFA Carpet → RERA Carpet: × 12/11 ≈ 1.0909** (RERA is ~9% larger than MOFA for same flat)
- Example: 35 sqm MOFA carpet × 1.2 = 42 sqm BUA

**Standard Conversion Relationships:**
| Relationship | Formula | Factor |
|---|---|---|
| MOFA Carpet → Built-Up | BUA = MOFA × 1.20 | 1.20 |
| Built-Up → MOFA Carpet | MOFA = BUA ÷ 1.20 | ÷1.20 |
| RERA Carpet → Built-Up | BUA = RERA × 1.10 | 1.10 |
| MOFA Carpet → RERA Carpet | RERA = MOFA × (12/11) | ≈1.0909 |

**Drawing-level risk**: The 1.20 equivalence holds when the ratio is consistent. Actual ratios may deviate due to varying wall thicknesses (230mm/115mm), structural columns near lift cores, service ducts, and irregular layouts. If actual ratio exceeds 1.20, promising 35% on MOFA creates shortfall from saleable inventory. Safer for developers to anchor agreements to BUA.

### Fungible Area
- 35% additional on BUA (not counted in FSI)
- Applied AFTER BUA conversion: BUA × 1.35 = Total BUA (including fungible)
- Total BUA ÷ 1.2 = MOFA carpet (since BUA = MOFA × 1.2)
- Premium: 50% of Ready Reckoner (RR) rate on sale component
- Free on rehab component (most schemes)
- Free for EWS/LIG tenements ≤ 35 sqm carpet
- Example: 42 sqm BUA × 1.35 = 56.7 sqm Total BUA → 56.7 ÷ 1.2 = 47.25 sqm MOFA carpet
- Note: Since 1.2 cancels out, MOFA carpet = Entitlement Carpet × 1.35 (shortcut)

### Entitlement Calculation Methodology
The standard entitlement conversion follows this sequence:
1. Start with existing carpet area
2. Apply entitlement increase (35% for 33(5), or 5%/8%/15% for 33(7)/33(7A)/33(9))
3. Apply layout/additional area entitlement if applicable
4. Convert to BUA: × 1.2
5. Apply fungible on BUA: × 1.35 → gives Total BUA (with fungible)
6. Convert Total BUA to MOFA carpet: ÷ 1.2

**Equivalence (proven algebraically):**
Since BUA = Carpet × 1.2, and MOFA = Total BUA ÷ 1.2:
MOFA = (Carpet × 1.2 × 1.35) ÷ 1.2 = Carpet × 1.35
The 1.2 factor cancels out. 35% on BUA = 35% on MOFA carpet.

**Critical rule for minimum 35 sqm (33(5) MHADA):**
- IF existing carpet ≥ 35 sqm: Entitlement Carpet = existing × (1 + 35% + layout%) → ×1.2 (BUA) → ×1.35 (Total BUA) → ÷1.2 (MOFA)
- IF existing carpet < 35 sqm: Bumped to 35 sqm minimum, gets ONLY layout% (NOT the 35% entitlement) → ×1.2 → ×1.35 → ÷1.2

**The "82% effect" (33(5) with layout):**
When both 35% entitlement and 35% fungible apply: 1.35 × 1.35 = 1.8225 (≈82% increase on carpet area, before BUA conversion)

### Carpet Conversions
- MOFA carpet: BUA × 8.973 (for old agreements)
- RERA carpet: BUA × 9.789 (for new agreements)

### Reg 14(A) Deduction
- 5% of (Gross Plot Area - Road Setback Area) for amenities
- Applicable to certain schemes

### Net Plot Calculation
- Net Plot = Gross Plot - Road Setback - DP Reservations - 14(A) Deduction (where applicable)

### Ready Reckoner (RR) Rate
- Annual Statement of Rates (ASR) published by IGR
- Used for all premium calculations
- Land rate and construction rate both published annually

### LR/RC Ratio
- LR = Land Rate per sqm (from ASR)
- RC = Rate of Construction for RCC (from ASR)
- This ratio determines incentive percentages and sharing ratios in 33(5), 33(7), 33(9)

### Standard Premiums (applicable to most schemes)
- Fungible: 50% of RR (on sale)
- Open Space Deficiency: 10% of normal premium
- Staircase/Lift: 15% of construction cost
- Development Charges: as per MCGM rates
- CFO Fees: fire department compliance
- LUC (Land Under Construction): premium for land use change
- Scrutiny Fees: plan approval fees
- IOD Premium: Intimation of Disapproval charges

### Indirect Costs (not DCPR-regulated but standard in feasibility)
- Rent to tenants during construction
- Corpus fund (10 years maintenance)
- Shifting charges (move-in/move-out)
- Brokerage (2% of sale)
- DA/Stamp Duty (6-7% of sale)
- Marketing (1-2% of sale)
- Land cost (for private land acquisition)
- Interest on project cost (12-15% typical)
