# Regulation 30 — DCPR 2034

**Floor Space Indices & Floor Space / Built-Up Area (BUA) Computation, Tenement Density and Protected Development**

---

### 30(A)(2) — Basis of FSI (excluded lands)

Permissible FSI shall be on plot area **excluding**:

- Area under DP roads / roads for which a sanctioned Regular Line is prescribed under the MMC Act (per Regulation 16);
- Amenity plots under Regulation 14;
- Area of DP reservations to be surrendered to MCGM / Appropriate Authority under Regulation 17.

---

### 30(A)(3) — TDR treatment of surrendered land

**(a)** TDR under Regulation 32 Table 12(A), for land surrendered under 30(A)(2), may be consumed as FSI on the balance plot per column 6 of Table 12, within the admissible TDR limit — **except** where the surrender is under Accommodation Reservation (AR) under Regulation 17. Under AR, Note 20(vii) & (viii) of Regulation 17(1) applies instead.

*Proviso:* FSI equivalent to TDR under Table 12A of Regulation 32, in lieu of road widening / roads proposed under the DP or MMC Act 1888 (per Regulation 16), may be utilised **over and above** the permissible FSI in column 7 of Table 12 on the balance plot, **subject to overall FSI not exceeding 1.25 × permissible FSI of column 7**.

**(b)** TDR in lieu of cost of construction of built-up amenity to be handed over per Regulation 17(1) Note 1(d), and for amenities under Regulations 14, 15 & 17, is permissible within permissible BUA — or the owner may avail the TDR (if unconsumed on the balance plot) elsewhere. *(Added 21 Dec 2021.)*

**(c)** *[KEPT IN ABEYANCE]* If the owner / developer cannot consume even the Zonal (basic) FSI due to planning constraints, they are entitled to TDR for the unconsumed BUA (including sub-clauses (a) and (c) above). In that case, no additional-FSI-on-premium / TDR may be utilised on the balance plot.

### 30(A)(4) — Cotton Textile Mills

*[KEPT IN ABEYANCE, then finally sanctioned via modification to Reg 35(1)(b) and Reg 30(A)(4), UD-11, 13 April 2022.]*

The permissible FSI shall be on plot area **excluding** areas to be surrendered to MCGM / Appropriate Authority under Regulation 35 (development of cotton textile mills). Areas so surrendered shall be treated as **deemed reservations**.

*Proviso:* BUA in lieu of cost of construction of built-up amenity to be handed over to MCGM under Regulation 15, if any, is permissible over and above permissible BUA (column 7 of Table 12), or the owner may avail the TDR if not consumed on the balance plot. *[KEPT IN ABEYANCE]*

---

### 30(A)(5) — Consumption after handover

BUA arrived at under 30(A) shall be allowed to be consumed on the balance plot **after** the reservation / amenity plot / DP road / MMC-Act Regular Line road is handed over to MCGM, within the admissible limit.

---

### 30(A)(6) — Premium on Additional FSI

Premium is charged for the *additional FSI on payment of premium* (column 5 of Table 12) at **50% of the ASR land rate (for FSI 1.0)** of the year in which the FSI is granted, or as decided by Government from time to time.

Premium is shared **25 : 50 : 25** between State Government, MCGM, and Dharavi Authority. *(Amended 14 Oct 2024.)* MCGM shall utilise its share for DP implementation.

*Proviso:* Utilisation of *Additional FSI on Premium* and TDR is **optional** and may be utilised in any sequential manner, subject to the limits in columns 5 & 6 of Table 12. Additional FSI on Premium is **non-transferable**, granted on application and payment, and may be used **on the same plot only**.

---

### 30(A)(7) — Development Cess

A Development Cess at **100% of the Development Charge**, for BUA over and above the Zonal (basic) FSI / Protected BUA (whichever is higher — excluding fungible compensatory area), shall be paid per the Commissioner's schedule.

**Not applicable to:**

- BUA to be handed over to MCGM / Appropriate Authority;
- BUA excluded from FSI computation;
- Proposals of Government / MCGM executed departmentally;
- Development under Regulations **33(3), 33(3)(A), 33(3)(B), 33(5), 33(7), 33(7)(A), 33(9), 33(9)(B), 33(10), 33(11)** — for these, cess / off-site infrastructure charges apply per the concerned regulation.

This cess is **in addition** to development charges levied under Section 124 of the MR&TP Act 1966.

---

### 30(A)(8) — Advance possession of surrendered land

The owner shall give advance possession of the land to be handed over to MCGM / MHADA / Appropriate Authority at the time of seeking development permission. The owner is responsible for clearing all encumbrances and completing the land-record transfer.

---

### 30(A)(9) — Timeline for handover and FSI utilisation

The plot (free of encumbrances) shall be handed over within **24 months** of building-plan / layout approval. FSI of the surrendered plot may be used on the remainder plot only after:

- Handover to MCGM, **or**
- Before availing Zonal (basic) FSI beyond 75% of the plot area, **or**
- Before granting Occupation Certificate to any building —

whichever is earlier. Ownership must transfer to MCGM / MHADA / Appropriate Authority in Land Revenue Records before OC is sought for any building other than the amenity.

---

### 30(A)(10) — Built-up amenity in FSI

Built-up amenity area is counted in FSI initially; after handover, it becomes **free of FSI**. Commencement Certificate for BUA in lieu of built-up amenity to be handed over under AR is granted only after that amenity is handed over.

---

### 30(A)(11) — Fungible Compensatory Area

Fungible Compensatory Area / BUA is permissible **over and above** permissible FSI (column 7 of Table 12), per Regulation 31(3).

---

### 30(A)(12) — Combination of Regulations

Development under a **combination of regulations** is permissible, but total permissible FSI on the plot shall not exceed the FSI limit prescribed in the respective regulations.

---

### 30(A)(13) — No TDR for unused balance

TDR **is not permitted** in lieu of balance BUA after loading of *additional FSI on payment of premium* and *admissible TDR* per Table 12.

---

### 30(A)(14) — Premium applicability

For development under 30(A) or under Regulations **33(2), 33(3), 33(3)(A), 33(3)(B), 33(8)(B), 33(11), 33(20)(A)**, premium **applies to** Central Government and their statutory bodies / undertakings and State Government undertakings — **except** State Government and MCGM themselves.

---

### 30(A)(15) — Refund of unused premium *(added 26 Oct 2021)*

If the owner / developer does not utilise additional FSI sanctioned under DCR 1991 or DCPR 2034, and CC is not issued for it, the premium already paid is **refundable after deducting 10% administrative charges, without interest**. Other terms of the directive dated 14 March 2016 on premium under Section 154 of the MR&TP Act 1966 apply, as amended.

---

### General Notes (applicable to all of Regulation 30(A))

**(1)** Layout open-space area required under Regulation 27 shall **not** be deducted for BUA / FSI computation.

**(2)** In new development / redevelopment where the plot is affected by a Road Line / DP Road / Reservation, and where that affected land has either been merged or handed over to MCGM and its FSI benefit already availed, or monetary compensation claimed in the past **but ownership not yet transferred** — the plot area is reckoned **after deduction** of such areas.

**(3)** OC for buildings built from the **last 25% of admissible FSI** (residential / commercial) is granted only after the recreational area is developed and its structures actually provided on site. For layouts with 2+ buildings, proportionate recreational-area development may be permitted, subject to completion of the entire recreational area & structures prior to OC of the last building.

**(4)** For Government / Semi-Government bodies (Atomic Energy Dept, Civil Aviation Dept, AAI, Defence, Railways, Mumbai Port Trust), the following land is **excluded** from available-FSI computation (if not existing amenity / reserved but shown as such in the DP):

- (a) Play grounds, stadia, golf courses
- (b) Parade grounds, training grounds, firing ranges
- (c) Green areas within complexes
- (d) Lands kept open for operational purposes
- (e) Lands under major internal roads
- (f) Railway tracks and yards
- (g) Lands unauthorisedly reclaimed
- (h) Lands of air-fields and air-strips

## 30(B) Tenement Density

- **Maximum tenement density:** **450 per ha** for FSI 1.00 — increased / reduced proportionately with FSI.
- **Minimum tenement density for Rehabilitation & Resettlement / Affordable Housing plots:** **325 per ha** for FSI 1.00 — increased / reduced proportionately with FSI.

---

## 30(C) Protected Development

**(a)** The FSI in Table 12 may be **exceeded** for redevelopment of an existing authorised building — to the extent of the existing authorised development rights / BUA — and is also entitled to additional FSI under the relevant regulations.

*Proviso:* Where additional FSI was previously availed for **educational, medical, starred hotels, religious or IT establishments**, and redevelopment discontinues that use, the additional BUA is **not protected**. Development must conform to these Regulations. Premium paid earlier for the discontinued use is **not adjusted**.

**(b)** Where development is **incomplete**, the balance development rights permissible under these Regulations may be availed by utilising TDR or Additional FSI on Premium — adjusting past payments for FSI grants, concessions or condonations, but **no refund** is permitted.

*Proviso:* If development is proposed only to the extent of Protected BUA per (a), a **9 m road width** is considered adequate. If more than the Protected area is proposed, Regulation 19(2) restrictions apply.
